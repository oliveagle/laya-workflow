## Findings

- **Wire path works**: laya-workflow's `--base-url` reaches laya-tch's `/v1/systemone`. Every laya_mem spec that exercises the decision model produces a usable answer.
- **Heuristic vs live**: heuristic is a `match_any` substring scan (sharp, binary, ~5ms, confidence 0.9). Live model gives nuanced continuous scores with much lower confidence on short observations (0.0003-0.08) — exactly the design space BLOCK/CONFIRM/ALLOW was built for.
- **Latency**: ~870ms end-to-end through laya-tch on CPU (HTTP + forward pass + subprocess). Apps parity's 8/8 on `agent_gate` shows low-stakes decisions come in at ~530ms.
- **Apps parity**: 18/25 (72%) — perfect on security-sensitive commands (8/8), 5/6 on content moderation, 1/6 on draft-scorer (model over-rates drafts as `send_now`).
